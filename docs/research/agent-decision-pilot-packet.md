# Agent decision pilot 1: baseline decision packet, action menu and verification draft

**Status: draft for owner review, 2026-11-17; revised after Codex review the same day.** Prepared under the owner's ADR-009 authorization for the FL Studio feasibility work. The pilot asks the AI decision component (`decision_agent`, ADR-005) to choose one action from a bounded menu. That action is a **proposal only**: admission, rendering and verification stay deterministic (ADR-004).

This document stops before the agent is asked anything. The producer goal is the owner's to state. Every threshold below is a **pilot setting for review**, not a validated criterion.

**Scope of the evidence:**
- **Inputs:** only the three baseline exports (kick, 808, master) of `C:/uni/Bachelor-Thesis/test/trap-baseline`. The manual kick-cut candidate, its measurements and its result are not in the packet. The packet script opens only `trap-baseline_*.wav`, refuses a candidate prefix, and a test proves that a candidate file in the same folder is never opened.
- **What the measurements mean:** masking margins are dist10 **model exceedance** of a masker's threshold over a target's energy, not proven perceptual masking (ADR-006). No action is recommended here.

## 1. Producer goal (owner)

> **"make kick more dominant then bass"** (owner, 2026-11-17, verbatim).

- **Record:** `producer-goal.json` in the packet folder (SHA-256 `bebead07857dae561788173509a78e977da70c3837bf32482ab9db589280ee82`, re-recorded with unchanged text after the packet split in §2), kept separate from `decision-packet.json`. The agent receives these words unchanged.
- **Verification mapping** (deterministic side, not given to the agent): protected stem **P = kick** and covering stem **C = 808** (insert "Bass").
  - Primary direction 808 → kick: evidence set E = the frozen `evidence` list of 808 → kick in `verification-sets.json`, 206 cells, unrestricted because the goal names no regions or bands.
  - Other-stem guard: the owner-approved 808 level guard (§5). The kick → 808 margins over G (5,600 cells; at-risk subset G₀ = 5,483) are reported descriptively only.
- **Still needed before execution (§3):** the Kick and Bass insert indices and names, confirmed in the FL Studio mixer.

### Conflict between the goal and the drafted guard (resolved by the owner, 2026-11-17)

A gain cut moves the margins almost uniformly wherever the stem is well above rounding noise:
- **A 808 cut of g dB** lowers 808 → kick margins by about g, so the primary check passes. It also lowers the 808's energy, so kick → 808 margins over G rise by about g.
  - G's baseline median is −60.7 dB, mostly cells where the kick's threshold is only the threshold in quiet.
  - The guard's median rule (≤ +1.0 dB) therefore rejects A5 and A6 (−2 and −3 dB) and leaves A4 (−1 dB) on the edge.
  - The newly-exceeding rule does not bind: only 14, 20 and 29 of the 5,483 at-risk cells (0.26 %, 0.36 % and 0.53 %) lie within 1, 2 and 3 dB of 0 dB at baseline.
- **A kick cut** raises 808 → kick margins, so the primary check fails.

As drafted, the guard treats the 808 becoming more covered by the kick as a regression, although that is what the goal asks for. These baseline-only counts are for the owner's criteria review and are **not** given to the agent.

**Options:**
1. **Keep the guard as drafted.** Effectively only A4 can be accepted, and only marginally.
2. **Bound the intended shift instead of forbidding it** (proposed). For this goal the other-stem guard becomes: median Δ over G ≤ +**3.0 dB** (the largest menu step), and at most 10 % of G₀ newly exceeding. It then catches only changes beyond the menu's size or widespread new coverage of the 808.
3. **Replace the guard with an 808 level guard,** for example 808 stem RMS change ≥ −3.0 dB.

**Owner decision (2026-11-17, before the agent was run):** a level guard on the 808, `candidate 808 RMS − baseline 808 RMS ≥ −3.25 dB`. It is measured on the original stereo 808 stem export (`trap-baseline_Bass.wav` against the candidate's), with pooled-channel RMS (`levels.sample_rms_dbfs`). The reverse-direction (kick → 808) margin changes are reported descriptively, with no pass/fail rule. This replaces the +1.0 dB median guard of the earlier draft.

### 2.4 Where the evidence is uncertain

- **Silent windows:** target windows with no nonzero sample are excluded: 206 frames when the 808 is the target, 431 when the kick is. The FFT energy floor in those windows is not target activity.
- **Threshold in quiet:** an exceedance whose masker threshold is not elevated (7 in each direction) is the absolute threshold, not the masker. So is one whose target is already below the threshold in quiet (0 kick → 808, 2 808 → kick). Neither kind is evidence.
- **Low-energy bands (pilot setting, flag at 10 dB):** a cell is flagged when the target's or masker's band energy in a sounding window lies within 10 dB of the int16 rounding-noise reference. There, export and mono-conversion rounding can move margins by several dB.
  - 1,057 kick → 808 cells and 704 808 → kick cells are flagged.
  - In 808 → kick, most masker-dependent exceedances above about 0.8 kHz in the kick's decay are flagged (107 of 189 in bands 5–11, 207 of 227 in bands 12–20). That high-frequency 808 → kick evidence is weak.
  - Flagged cells are in neither frozen set.

## 5. Verification draft (tied to the producer goal)

The goal will name a **protected stem P** (to become clearer) and a **covering stem C**. The primary direction is then C → P: C's threshold over P's energy. Both cell sets are **frozen from the baseline packet before the agent chooses**: the `evidence` and `guard` lists per direction in `verification-sets.json`, each `[call, band, baseline margin]`, not given to the agent. They are not recomputed after the edit, and nor are their low-energy flags.

- **Evidence set E** = the frozen `evidence` list of direction C → P: masker-dependent and not flagged. If the goal names time regions or bands, the restriction is applied and recorded *before* the agent is asked. `n_E` = its size; every cell in it exceeds 0 dB at baseline.
- **Descriptive set G** = the frozen `guard` list of the reverse direction P → C: eligible, C's window not silent, not flagged. Its median Δ and the share of its at-risk cells G₀ (baseline margin ≤ 0 dB) that newly exceed are reported, **not judged** (owner decision; see §1).
- **Change per cell** Δ = margin after − margin before, on the same `[call, band]`.

| Check | Rule | Setting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary: goal** | median Δ over E ≤ −**1.0 dB**, and the cells of E with margin > 0 after the edit number fewer than `n_E` | 1.0 dB: pilot setting |
| **Guard: other stem (808 level)** | **candidate 808 RMS − baseline 808 RMS ≥ −3.25 dB**, pooled-channel RMS of the original stereo 808 stem exports | −3.25 dB: owner-approved pilot setting |
| Reverse direction (descriptive) | median Δ over G and share of G₀ newly exceeding, reported only | no rule |
| **Guard: master level** | candidate master sample peak ≤ **−1.0 dBFS**, and **candidate RMS − baseline RMS ≥ −1.5 dB** (pooled channels, stereo master) | −1.0 dBFS, −1.5 dB: pilot settings |
| **Guard: uncertainty** | flagged cells and cells outside E and G are reported separately and count neither for nor against the action | rule; the 10 dB flag is a pilot setting |
| Integrity | §4 checks pass; insert identity and the §3 execution tolerance confirmed | exact |

**Edge cases:**
- **Empty evidence set** (`n_E = 0`): the baseline evidence does not support the goal direction. Only A0 can then be verified (as a correct abstention); any other action is *not verified: no evidence set*.
- **808 level guard unavailable** (silent or unreadable 808 export): any action other than A0 is *not verified*.
- **Unusable cells after the edit:** a frozen cell becomes unusable if its target window becomes exactly silent (an edit can round tiny samples to zero) or its margin becomes undefined (zero threshold or zero target energy). Unusable cells are never dropped or imputed. If any cell of E is unusable, the result is *not verified*, with those cells listed. Unusable cells of G are listed in the descriptive report.
- **Abstention (A0):** verified if the baseline project and exports are unchanged (hashes) and a reason is recorded.

**Outcome:**
- *Accept* only if the primary check and every guard pass.
- *Roll back* (keep the baseline) if the primary check or any guard fails.
- *Not verified* if execution, integrity or an edge case above prevents the checks.

**Caveats:**
- **Mechanical result.** A cut on C lowers C's threshold almost uniformly, by about Δ where C is well above rounding noise. A cut on P lowers P's energy, which *raises* the C → P margins. So the primary check mainly tests that the chosen action has the right *sign and size* for the goal, and the guards carry most of the information.
- **Not a listening result.** Whether the mix sounds better is left to a separate listening comparison. Model exceedance is not perceptual masking.
